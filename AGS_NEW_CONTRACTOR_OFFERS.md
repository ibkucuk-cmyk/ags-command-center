# AGS New Contractor Switching Offers

> **Last Updated:** 2026-05-15
> **Purpose:** Creative, low-cost incentive package to attract new contractors away from their current stone fabricator.
> **Rule:** All offers must comply with `STRATEGY_RULES.md` — no price competition, no misleading claims, no channel conflict.

## The Core Starter Package

> **"AGS New Partner Starter Package"**

|---|---|---|

## Offer Details

### 1. Remnant Program — "Free Stone on Your First 3 Jobs"

**What:** Contractors switching to AGS get free remnant material on their first 3 vanity, bar top, or table top jobs. They pay fabrication + install only.

**Why it works:**
- Contractors switching fabricators face risk — *"what if the new guy screws up?"* Free material eliminates the financial risk of trying AGS.
- Remnants are **sunk cost** — they're already sitting in the yard generating $0. Turning them into a customer acquisition tool is pure upside.
- You still make margin on fabrication + install labor.

**Positioning language:**
> *"We'd rather these slabs become your client's countertop than sit in our yard."*

**Best segments for this offer:**
- **Apartment complexes / senior living** — bathroom vanity renovations (bulk, recurring)
- **Restaurant fit-out contractors** — bar tops, table tops
- **Flippers / investors** — fast-cash rehab jobs (segment recovery play)
- **Small/medium GCs** — spec home vanities

**Guard rail:** Cap at 3 jobs or $X in material value (whichever comes first). After that, they're buying at regular pricing — but by then they've experienced AGS quality and are locked in.

---

### 2. "Pro Portal Priority Access" — Exclusive Early Access

**What:** Position Pro Portal access as a limited, invite-only benefit for new partners.

**Pitch language:**
> *"We're onboarding 10 new Pro Partners this quarter with early access to our self-serve quoting portal. Your competitors are still calling estimating departments and waiting 3 days."*

**Why it works:**
- Creates urgency and exclusivity (scarcity psychology)
- Puts AGS's #1 tech differentiator front and center on the first interaction
- Free — no incremental cost
- Directly attacks Classic and Artelye's weakness (slow estimating departments)

---

### 3. "Showroom as Your Showroom" — Client Meeting Space

**What:** Offer new contractors free use of the AGS indoor showroom to bring their clients for material selection.

**Pitch language:**
> *"Bring your homeowner to our indoor slab yard. We'll walk them through options, help them pick, and close the deal — you look like a pro, and we handle the material side."*

**Why it works:**
- Massive value-add that costs AGS nothing
- Competitors with outdoor yards can't offer this experience in rain/heat/cold
- **Especially powerful for K&B stores and designers** who don't have slab inventory on-site
- The contractor looks premium in front of their client — that builds loyalty to AGS

---

### 4. "Net-30 Starter Terms" — No Credit App Required

**What:** New contractors get Net-30 payment terms on their first 3 jobs without going through a formal credit application.

**Why it works:**
- Removes the *"I have to fill out credit applications and wait for approval"* friction that kills switching momentum
- Shows trust and partnership posture upfront

**Guard rails:**
- Cap outstanding receivables at **$5,000** during the trial period
- After 3 jobs, transition to standard credit terms based on payment history
- Target this at **fast-paying segments** (K&B stores, flippers, small GCs) — NOT at large GCs who will naturally push to 60-90 days anyway

## Additional Offers (Pick One for 5th Slot)

### 5a. "Priority Scheduling on Job #1"

**What:** Bump the new contractor's first project to the front of the fabrication queue.

**Why it works:**
- First impressions define the relationship. If their current fabricator is slow (and they probably are — that's why they're looking), a fast first turnaround seals the deal.
- Costs you nothing except minor schedule juggling.

### 5b. "Free Sink Cutout on Job #1"

**What:** Waive the undermount sink cutout charge on the contractor's first job.

**Why it works:**
- Tangible dollar savings they'll see on the invoice (~$50-150 depending on cutout type)
- Small enough to not hurt margin, big enough to notice

### 5c. "Dedicated Point of Contact"

**What:** Assign a specific person (founder or partner) as their dedicated contact for the first 3 jobs.

**Why it works:**
- They experience the "Direct Access to Ownership" promise from day one
- Builds the personal relationship that prevents them from price-shopping later
- Directly delivers on the competitive counter-play vs. Artelye and Classic (bureaucratic, impersonal)

## Referral Multiplier — "Buddy Referral"

**What:** Existing B2B partners who refer a new contractor account get a **credit on their next job** ($250–$500). The referred contractor gets the Starter Package above.

**Why it works:**
- Low-cost acquisition — your existing happy partners become your sales force
- Leverages AGS's strongest asset: 20 years of word-of-mouth reputation
- The referring partner has skin in the game — they're vouching for AGS, which makes the new contractor more likely to commit

## ⚠️ What We Will NOT Offer (Per Strategy Rules)

| Prohibited Offer | Why |
|---|---|
| Deep discounts on premium full slabs | We don't compete on price. That's United's game. Discounting trains contractors to always ask for discounts. |
| Free cabinets / bundled product lines | Channel conflict risk with K&B store partners. Violates Pillar 4 rules. |
| Guaranteed install dates / timelines | Per messaging guardrails — delays happen, we communicate proactively but don't make promises we can't keep. |
| Any reference to financial strength or "backed inventory" | Misleading given debt reality. Never reference financial strength. |
| Extended Net-60/90 terms as incentive | Worsens cash flow — contradicts Pillar 3 (Cash Flow Defense). Only offer Net-30 max for trial. |
