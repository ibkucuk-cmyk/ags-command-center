# AGS Competitive Teardown & Gap Analysis

> **Last Updated:** 2026-05-14
> Part of Phase 5 (Digital B2B Strategy). Identifies competitive gaps to exploit in outbound sales and Pro Portal positioning.

---

## 1. Classic Granite & Marble (Jessup, MD)
* **Profile:** ~20-year pedigree, robotic sawjets, large operation. Targets the same medium/large contractors and K&B stores as AGS.
* **Inventory:** 2,000–5,000 slabs. Massive yard.
* **The Threat:** They have scale, tech, and the same market credibility AGS has.
* **Their Weakness:** Big inventory = big overhead = bureaucratic estimating and quoting processes. When a contractor needs a fast, flexible partner, Classic's size works against them.
* **AGS Counter-Play:** **Speed and self-serve access.** AGS offers the Pro Portal where B2B partners build their own quotes instantly — no waiting on a sales rep or estimating department. We are the agile alternative to a slow-moving big shop.

---

## 2. JPS Granite (Edgewood & Forest Hill, MD)
* **Profile:** Local competitor literally in AGS's backyard. Offers cabinets and plumbing on top of stone — positions as a "one-stop-shop" for smaller contractors.
* **Pricing:** Cheaper than AGS.
* **Quality:** Not good. Uses **subcontractors** for installs, which means they lose control of the final product.
* **The Threat:** They're close geographically and their lower pricing appeals to cost-conscious small contractors.
* **Their Weakness:** Diluted focus (cabinets + plumbing + stone), subcontracted labor, and quality issues show up in mixed reviews.
* **AGS Counter-Play:** **100% in-house quality control.** When a GC is putting their reputation on a project — especially a large commercial or high-end residential job — they can't risk a subcontractor botching the install. AGS does everything in-house. The price difference is the cost of reliability.

---

## 3. United Granite (The "Blue Sign" Location)
* **Profile:** Low-cost leader. Shares the same contractor base as AGS. Friendly competition — they're nearby and contractors cross-shop purely on price.
* **Quality:** Was lower quality in the past. **May have improved** — needs current intel.
* **The Threat:** Price wins deals with budget-conscious contractors and flippers. If their quality has actually improved, the "premium vs. budget" argument weakens.
* **Their Weakness (Honest Assessment):** The old "they're cheap and unreliable" script may no longer hold if they've upgraded their quality. We cannot sell against them with outdated assumptions.
* **AGS Counter-Play:** Don't try to out-script them. Instead:
  1. **Gather current intel** — have we lost specific jobs to United recently? What's the feedback from contractors who use both?
  2. **Position on relationship, not just quality** — AGS provides partner-level attention, dedicated project management, and the self-serve Pro Portal. United is transactional; AGS is a partnership.
  3. **Don't compete on price.** If a contractor is choosing purely on price, they're not our customer.

> ⚠️ **ACTION NEEDED:** We need real, current competitive intelligence on United. The old assumptions may be stale.

---

## 4. Artelye Marble & Granite (Beltsville, MD)
* **Profile:** Established since 1996. Strong commercial ties in the DC market.
* **Inventory:** 2,000–5,000 slabs (comparable to Classic — large operation).
* **The Threat:** DC commercial market is slowing down. Artelye will get hungry and start pushing north into AGS's MD/PA/DE territory.
* **Their Weakness:** As they expand north, they'll treat MD contractors as secondary accounts — less attention, slower response, just another number. They don't have local relationships in our territory.
* **AGS Counter-Play:** **Personalized, local partner attention.** When Artelye shows up offering DC-level scale, AGS counters with: "We're your local partner. Direct access to ownership. Same-day responses. You're not account #347 — you're a partner." Combined with the Pro Portal for instant self-serve quoting, we offer both the personal touch AND the tech.

## Conclusion & Messaging Framework for Pro Portal

Our B2B positioning must be **honest and defensible**. No puffery. These are the 4 messages that map to real competitive gaps:

| # | Message | Hits | Why It's True |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Build Your Own Quotes Instantly via the Pro Portal."** | Classic, Artelye (slow quoting) | Self-serve portal eliminates waiting on estimating departments |
| 2 | **"100% In-House. No Subcontractors. Every Install."** | JPS (subcontracts installs) | AGS controls quality from slab selection to final install |
| 3 | **"Direct Access to Ownership. Partner-Level Attention."** | Artelye (will treat MD as secondary), United (transactional) | Two-partner shop where you talk to the decision-makers |
| 4 | **"20 Years. Indoor Slab Yard. No Compromises."** | All | Credibility signal — not a startup, not cutting corners |

### Messaging Rules (What We Will NOT Say)
* ❌ ~~"Walk our 500+ Indoor Slab Yard"~~ — Don't lead with slab count. We have ~500 slabs; competitors have 2,000-5,000. Lead with **indoor** and **quality of experience**, not quantity.
* ❌ ~~"Quotes in 24 Hours"~~ — Undersells the real advantage. The Pro Portal lets partners **build their own quotes instantly**. Don't promise a timeframe — promise self-serve control.
* ❌ ~~"Guaranteed Install Dates"~~ — We can't guarantee this. Delays happen (late stone orders). We always communicate proactively, but making a guarantee we can't keep damages trust.
* ❌ ~~"Financial backing to secure inventory upfront"~~ — Meaningless and potentially misleading given our financial reality. Never reference financial strength as a selling point.
* ❌ Don't sell against United with outdated quality assumptions. Get current intel first.
