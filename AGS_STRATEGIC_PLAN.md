## Situation Analysis

### Why Revenue Is Flat Despite New Wins
AGS added two major GC accounts in the last 2 years (**Harkins Builders** and **CCS Building Group**), yet top-line revenue remained flat. Three offsetting forces explain this:

1. **Flipper/Investor Segment Collapse:** The investor rehab market that used to drive fast-cash volume has dried up significantly.
2. **Lost Designer Account:** A meaningful designer relationship ended, taking both direct revenue and the referral pipeline with it.
3. **General Market Softness:** Existing B2B accounts (contractors, K&B stores) are flowing fewer projects through due to a slower overall construction/renovation market.

**The math:** New accounts are replacing lost revenue, not growing it. We need a strategy that generates **net-new growth** above the replacement rate.

## The Strategic Architecture (5 Pillars)

### Pillar 1: Frictionless B2B Onboarding (Freeing The Founder's Time)

**The Problem:** Every new GC relationship requires extensive qualification paperwork — OSHA documents, safety manuals, insurance certs, W9s, company history, references. The founder is the only person who can complete these, and he's stretched across AGS, Autevo, Niona, and personal obligations. AGS is currently "flirting" with multiple new GCs but the paperwork bottleneck slows or kills deals.

**The Solution:**
* **"Master GC Packet" Automation:** Create a standardized, pre-filled master document set that contains 80% of the data every GC asks for. Only GC-specific fields need manual entry.
* **Delegation / Autevo Integration:** Train the business partner or an admin to handle the remaining 20%. Long-term, build this into Autevo as a reusable onboarding workflow.
* **Template Library:** Maintain a living library of completed packets (Harkins template, CCS template, Generic GC template) so each new GC is a copy-edit, not a blank-page exercise.

**Success Metric:** Reduce GC onboarding time from days/weeks to < 2 hours of founder time per new account.

### Pillar 2: Low-Cost B2B Outbound (Targeting Fast-Paying Segments)

**The Problem:** GCs are high-value but slow-paying (60-90 days). We can't build the entire growth strategy on 90-day receivables when carrying high-interest debt. We need to **balance the portfolio** with faster-paying customer types.

**Target Segments (Priority Order by Cash Conversion Speed):**

| Priority | Segment | Why | Payment Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 High | Kitchen & Bath Stores | Fast cash, recurring, relationship-sticky | 50% upfront / 50% on completion |
| 🔴 High | Flippers / Investors (recovery) | Fast cash, volume | Net 15-30 or COD |
| 🟡 Medium | Interior Designers | High margin, referral network | 30-60 days |
| 🟡 Medium | Apartment Complexes | Bulk, recurring | Contract-dependent |
| 🟡 Medium | Retirement / Senior Living | Bulk, recurring | Contract-dependent |
| 🟢 Ongoing | Medium/Large GCs | Revenue anchor | 60-90 days (balance carefully) |

**The Playbook:**
* **Multi-channel outbound (NOT LinkedIn-only):**
  * **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — for medium/large GCs only. Small contractors aren't on LinkedIn.
  * **Google Maps cold calls** — for small contractors (guy-in-a-van segment). They have Google Business listings even without websites.
  * **K&B store walk-ins** — in-person visits with a "Why AGS" leave-behind one-pager.
  * **Email outreach via Instantly.ai** — cold email sequence from `agspartners.com` secondary domain. Works for all segments.
  * **Houzz/Instagram DMs** — for interior designers who live on visual platforms.
* **Lead with the value prop:** 20-year reputation, indoor slab yard, in-house quality control (no subcontractors), and the Self-Serve Pro Portal (powered by Autevo).
* **Target list:** Build a list of 50+ local K&B stores and designers as the immediate outbound focus.
* **Leverage existing wins:** Use Harkins and CCS as social proof / case studies in outbound messaging.

**Outbound Content Created (2026-05-15):**
* `AGS_WHY_AGS_ONEPAGER.md` — Print-ready one-pager for leave-behinds

### Pillar 4: Smart Showroom Expansion (Channel Conflict Mitigation)

**The Problem:** AGS has 2,000 sqft of empty adjacent space. Expanding the showroom with new product lines would increase foot traffic and wallet share. **BUT** some B2B partners sell the same products — adding them could trigger channel conflict and cost us accounts.

**Decision Framework:**
Before adding ANY product line, answer:
1. **Revenue at risk:** Which current B2B partners sell this product? What is their annual spend with AGS? Would they reduce or pull business if AGS starts selling it directly?
2. **Revenue to gain:** What incremental revenue can we realistically capture from this product? What new customer types does it attract?
3. **Net impact:** Only proceed if Gain > Risk by a meaningful margin.

**Positioning Strategy:**
* Frame the expanded showroom as an **"Extension of Your Showroom"** for B2B partners — a place they can bring their clients to see materials in a premium indoor setting.
* Products that **complement** (not compete with) existing partner offerings are safest: e.g., premium edge profiles, specialty stones, outdoor kitchen materials, porcelain slabs.
* Avoid commodity products that K&B stores already sell at volume.

### Pillar 5: Digital B2B Conversion & Positioning (Website Overhaul)

**The Problem:** The current website (`advancedgranite.com`) is built for residential trust signals (HomeAdvisor badges, Angi reviews). It doesn't speak to B2B buyers and lacks a dedicated intake funnel for professionals.

**The Solution — "AGS Pro Partner Portal":**

1. **Dedicated B2B Landing Page**
   * Headline messaging built from honest competitive counter-plays (see `AGS_COMPETITIVE_ANALYSIS.md` → Messaging Guardrails):
     - *"Build Your Own Quotes Instantly via the Pro Portal."* — Self-serve, no waiting on estimating departments
     - *"100% In-House. No Subcontractors. Every Install."* — Quality control differentiator vs. JPS
     - *"Direct Access to Ownership. Partner-Level Attention."* — Two-partner shop, not a bureaucracy
     - *"20 Years. Indoor Slab Yard. No Compromises."* — Credibility + experience quality (NOT slab count)
   * Visual emphasis on the indoor showroom experience, digital templating tech, and 20-year track record.

2. **B2B Social Proof Swap**
   * On the Pro Portal path, replace consumer reviews with:
     - B2B case studies (anonymized if needed)
     - Partner logos (Harkins, CCS — with permission)
     - Volume/project stats

3. **Self-Serve Quoting via Pro Portal**
   * B2B partners **build their own quotes** through the portal — select materials, input dimensions, get pricing instantly.
   * This is the core competitive differentiator vs. Classic and Artelye, where partners wait days for an estimating department to respond.
   * The portal doesn't just collect leads — it gives partners **control**.

4. **SEO & Local Search (B2B Keywords)**
   * Target: "commercial stone fabricator Maryland", "B2B granite supplier MD", "contractor countertop partner"
   * This is organic/free — no paid ads.
