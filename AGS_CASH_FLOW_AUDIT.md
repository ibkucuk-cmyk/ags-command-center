## Key Findings

> [!WARNING]
> At **~40% APR** on short-term loans, almost ANY alternative is cheaper:
> - Factoring at 3% on a 90-day invoice = ~12% annualized → **saves 28 percentage points**
> - Early payment discount of 2/15 Net 90 = ~9.7% annualized → **saves 30 percentage points**
> - Even expensive factoring at 5% = ~20% annualized → **still saves 20 percentage points**
>
> **The math overwhelmingly supports both factoring AND early payment discounts as cheaper alternatives to current debt.**
